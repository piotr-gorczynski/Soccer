# Google Play Store Description

## App Name
Gridline Soccer

## Short Description (<=80 characters)
Classic paper soccer reborn with online duels, tournaments, and smart AI.

## Full Description
Gridline Soccer brings the schoolyard paper soccer battle to your phone with a modern multiplayer twist. Sketch your passes one grid at a time, bounce off sidelines for clever angles, and fire the ball into the goal. Play shoulder‑to‑shoulder on one device, face friends online, or challenge the built‑in Android opponent when you want a quick match.

**Why you'll love it**
- ⚽ Authentic paper-soccer feel with smooth animations and responsive controls.
- 👥 Multiplayer your way: same‑device play, friend invites, or quick online matchups.
- 🧠 Solo mode: sharpen tactics against the Android AI any time.
- 🏆 Tournaments & rankings: compete for the leaderboard and track your progress.
- 🔔 Smart invites: get notified about missed friend challenges so you never skip a game.
- 🛡️ Safe community: AI-powered name filtering keeps lobbies friendly.
- 🌍 Multilingual: enjoy the experience across many supported languages.

Line up your shot, bank it off the wall, and outsmart your rival—every move is a fresh chance to score.
