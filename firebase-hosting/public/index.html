Paper soccer, friends and skill-based tournaments.

Play and compete

Play against Android, challenge friends and join available tournaments. Plan your moves, follow the game rules and work your way up the rankings.

Tournaments

Read and accept the assigned tournament rules before joining. Each event's rules describe eligibility, gameplay, deadlines and scoring. Results are determined by gameplay and the assigned rules. Entry is free, without a purchase, entry fee, deposit, stake, wager or payment from players. Available events and schedules may vary.

Already have an account?

Sign in with your existing Gridline Soccer account to access your profile and friend list. Global and Bangladesh share account and profile data. You cannot use both editions on the same device. Uninstalling an app is different from deleting your shared account.

Play respectfully

Choose an appropriate nickname and respect other players. You can report a player or nickname and block individual players using the player menu in the app.

Availability and support

Download Gridline Soccer from Google Play. Availability depends on your Google Play account and device. For help, visit Support.
