Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-13 · GLOBAL-privacy-2026-10-13

Introduction

This policy covers Gridline Soccer Global (piotr_gorczynski.soccer2), operated by Piotr Górczyński in Poland. Bangladesh has a separate Privacy Policy. We use Google AdMob for advertising and Firebase for authentication and operation of the service.

Information We Collect

Depending on the sign-in method, we process an internal account identifier (UID), nickname, email address and available provider profile information such as name and picture. Anonymous sign-in has a UID but does not require an email address.

We store your game statistics, tournament participation and presence status (online/offline) in Firebase Firestore and Realtime Database. The app also saves a Firebase Cloud Messaging token on your device and in Firestore to deliver push notifications.

Usage events and diagnostic breadcrumbs can describe navigation, authentication, tournament activity, app variant, environment and version. We use these to understand usage, investigate faults and improve the service.

Additionally, the app uses Google Analytics for Firebase (GA4) and Firebase Crashlytics. GA4 helps us understand how users engage with the app, while Crashlytics collects crash reports and diagnostic data (such as device type, operating system, app version, and crash details) to help us improve stability and fix problems. These services are provided by Google LLC in accordance with their Privacy Policy.

How We Use Information

We use your information to create and manage your account, show leaderboards, enable multiplayer matches and send you notifications. Your data also helps us detect abuse and keep the Service secure.

We use analytical data and breadcrumbs to analyze user behavior, improve app performance, fix bugs, and enhance the overall user experience. This data helps us understand how users interact with the app and identify areas for improvement.

Advertising

The app uses Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob may collect device information (such as your advertising ID) to serve ads. You can choose whether ads are personalized based on your consent and device settings. Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy.

Third-Party Services

We rely on Firebase services (Authentication, Firestore, Realtime Database, Cloud Messaging, Analytics and Crashlytics) provided by Google LLC. Authentication providers (e.g. Google or Facebook) may process your data according to their own privacy policies.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Google Firebase and AdMob, and your chosen sign-in provider, process information to operate the service; authorized administrators handle reports and support. Nicknames and gameplay results are visible to other players. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or necessary to handle a legal claim. Provider infrastructure may process information outside your country.

Shared accounts and legal evidence

Email/password, Google, Facebook and anonymous sign-in use an internal UID. Available profile data depends on the provider; anonymous sign-in does not require email. Global and Bangladesh share an account/profile. We record variants used, first/latest use and whether Bangladesh use followed Global; this does not establish location or Google Play country. We also check whether the other Gridline Soccer package is installed.

Terms acceptance records contain UID, scope, version, document URL/hash, language, timestamp and app context. Tournament-rule acceptance is stored with participation records. These document accepted rules and are separate from advertising consent.

Player reporting and blocking

A per-user block list stores blocked UIDs and creation timestamps to prevent direct invitations and friend additions involving a blocked player. Its owner and authorized administrators can access it. Reports contain reporter UID, reported UID, nickname snapshot, reason, context, status, timestamps and handling history. Only the reporter and authorized administrators can access the report. The purpose is abuse prevention, moderation and dispute handling; reporting does not automatically ban or punish anyone. Avoid unnecessary personal data in reports.

Moderation and legal-record retention

Blocks remain until unblock/account deletion. Open reports remain until resolved/closed. Scheduled cleanup deletes moderation reports and all their history/subcollections 180 days after resolution/closure. A legal/security hold can postpone deletion; releasing it does not reset deadlines. Overdue records are deleted on a successful cleanup run.

General Terms acceptance evidence is preserved for five calendar years from account deletion, then deleted with its subcollections by scheduled cleanup. The evidence itself is not modified to set expiry. The minimal user record and historical gameplay UIDs remain. Tournament-rule evidence in participation records is outside this cleanup and is not assigned the same five-year deadline. Bangladesh payout retention does not apply to Global moderation or legal acceptance data.

Your Choices

Where available, use the app’s privacy/consent options to reject, accept or withdraw analytics and advertising consent. Settings cover analytics storage, ad storage, advertising user data and ad personalization. Withdrawal does not undo earlier lawful processing and does not necessarily disable separate crash diagnostics. Android also provides advertising and notification settings. Contact us to request access, correction or deletion, or exercise other applicable rights. We may need proportionate information to verify a request; never send passwords or verification codes.

Account Deletion

Choose Remove Account on the account screen or request deletion at support@piotr-gorczynski.com. We delete the Firebase Authentication account and remove direct profile identifiers, including email, provider IDs/names/photos and notification tokens. The shared account is removed from both editions; uninstalling the app does not delete it.

A minimal record remains with the same UID, deletion marker/date and “(Account removed)” display name. Historical matches, tournament participation/results and rankings retain UID references for integrity. These data are pseudonymous, not necessarily anonymous. Screens resolve the removed-account name; offline devices update after reconnecting. This does not erase every historical record or previously captured information.

We deactivate presence and remove pending invitations, active friendships in both directions, the account’s blocks and internal moderation state, and its UID from others’ block lists. A minimal offline/deleted marker prevents old sessions restoring presence. Cleanup may require retries and is not simultaneous across services. Reports and legal evidence follow the periods below; other necessary game history remains. Contact us to review remaining information no longer needed.

Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal data, please contact us so we can take action.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Please review it periodically.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@piotr-gorczynski.com.
