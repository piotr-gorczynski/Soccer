Gridline Soccer Bangladesh

Paper soccer, friends and skill-based tournaments.

For adults aged 18 or older only. This section describes the Bangladesh edition of Gridline Soccer, developed by Piotr Górczyński.

Play and compete

Play against Android, challenge friends and join available tournaments. Where a tournament offers cash prizes, its own rules describe the prize pool, eligible places, tie handling and supported payout methods. Prizes are organizer-funded; participation requires no entry fee, stake, wager, deposit, purchase or payment. Current payout wallets are bKash and Rocket. Prizes and schedules vary by tournament.

How tournaments and payouts work

Already have an account?

You can use your existing Gridline Soccer account. The Global and Bangladesh editions share account and profile data, including your friend list. You cannot use both editions on the same device: to use Bangladesh, uninstall the Global edition, identified by its icon without the Bangladesh flag. Uninstalling an app is different from deleting your shared account.

Availability

Production distribution of the Bangladesh edition is intended for Bangladesh through Google Play country targeting. Google Play determines availability for your account and device. This website does not check or infer your location.
