Your account

Prizes

A prize depends on the final result and the event's prize rules. Prizes are organizer-funded, not funded by players. Supported mobile-money payout channels are bKash and Rocket. These details must correspond to the receiving wallet and Remitly recipient details; incorrect information can delay or prevent payment. Payouts are currently processed manually through Remitly and may require correction of recipient details. Check the app for progress and issue messages; contact support if something is wrong.

We do not promise instant payment or a fixed processing time in these general terms. The tournament's published rules describe its prize conditions. See the payout guide for the meaning of payment statuses.
