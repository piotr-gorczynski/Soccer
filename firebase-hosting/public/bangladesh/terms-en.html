Terms of Service — Bangladesh

Scope and operator

These draft terms concern Gridline Soccer Bangladesh (Android package piotr_gorczynski.soccer2.bd), provided by Piotr Górczyński. They cover use of the Bangladesh edition; each tournament also has its own rules presented before registration.

Age and eligibility

The Bangladesh edition is intended for adults aged 18 or older. You must meet the eligibility requirements stated for any tournament you enter and provide truthful confirmations. Availability through Google Play does not by itself establish tournament eligibility.

Your account

Keep your sign-in credentials secure. Do not impersonate another person or use offensive, discriminatory, threatening or misleading nicknames. Avoid putting your full name, email address or other personal information in a public nickname.

Your account is shared with the Global edition. Deleting the shared account affects access to both editions. To use Bangladesh on a device that also has Global installed, uninstall Global; you do not need to delete your account to change editions.

Tournaments

Read and accept the assigned tournament rules before joining. Those rules specify the event's eligibility, gameplay, deadlines, scoring and prize distribution. Cash-prize entry is free, without a purchase, entry fee, deposit or stake. This page does not guarantee a tournament schedule or prize amount.

Follow the game rules and do not cheat, exploit defects or interfere with other players. Match clocks and the event's rules determine the consequences of leaving a match, disconnecting or remaining inactive.

Prizes

A prize depends on the final result and the event's prize rules. Winners must provide correct recipient details for an offered payout method. Payouts are processed manually through a transfer provider and may require correction of recipient details. Check the app for progress and issue messages; contact support if something is wrong.

We do not promise instant payment or a fixed processing time in these general terms. The tournament's published rules describe its prize conditions. See the payout guide for the meaning of payment statuses.

Privacy and support

See the Bangladesh privacy policy for information about account, gameplay, payout and support data. Questions about these terms or a tournament can be sent to support@piotr-gorczynski.com.
