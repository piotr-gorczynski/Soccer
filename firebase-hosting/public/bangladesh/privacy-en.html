Privacy Policy — Bangladesh

Who this policy covers

This draft describes data handling in Gridline Soccer Bangladesh (piotr_gorczynski.soccer2.bd), provided by Piotr Górczyński. Contact: support@piotr-gorczynski.com. The Bangladesh edition is intended for adults aged 18 or older.

Account and gameplay information

Depending on how you sign in, we process an account identifier, email address, nickname and information supplied by your authentication provider, such as a name or profile picture. We store game results, tournament participation, friend connections and online presence to provide gameplay, rankings and invitations. Other players can see the nickname and gameplay information displayed in those features.

The Global and Bangladesh editions use shared account and profile records. We also record which app variants an account has used. This is usage tracking, not a determination of your Google Play country or physical location.

Tournament confirmations

For cash-prize registration, we record acceptance of the assigned tournament rules, confirmation of the required minimum age and confirmation that you have an account for a supported payout method, together with a timestamp and the relevant requirements. This registration flow does not request an identity-document upload.

Prize payments and support

When you submit payout details, we store the selected method and recipient account information, such as a mobile-wallet account number. Payment records include the prize, status, timestamps, issue messages and transfer provider/reference where recorded. The history can include previous recipient details and administrator changes.

If you submit a support ticket, we store its category, your message, replies, status and handling history. These records help the administrator arrange or correct payments and respond to issues. Payout details are not intended for public display.

To make a transfer, the administrator provides the necessary recipient information to the transfer provider and receiving payment service. Those providers process information under their own policies. The final published policy must identify the providers actually used and link to their policies.

Notifications, diagnostics, analytics and ads

Firebase Cloud Messaging uses installation and messaging identifiers to deliver notifications. Google Analytics for Firebase processes usage events and app/device information, including app variant, environment and version. Events include navigation, authentication, tournament views, prize views and installed-variant conflict prompts. The dedicated prize-view events do not include payout account details or support-message content.

Firebase Crashlytics processes crash reports and diagnostic information. Google AdMob processes information used to serve ads, which may include advertising identifiers and device information. The app uses Google's consent flow where applicable and applies its consent state to Analytics and advertising. This does not mean all diagnostic processing stops when an advertising choice changes.

The app checks whether the other Gridline Soccer package is installed to prevent both editions being used on the same device. It does not use that check to determine your country.

Service providers and access

We use Google Firebase for authentication, databases, messaging, analytics and crash reporting, and Google AdMob for advertising. Google and Facebook sign-in also involve the selected provider. See Google's privacy policy and Meta's privacy policy.

Authorized administrators handle payout and support records. The app uses authenticated access and database access rules to restrict access to protected records. Provider infrastructure may process information outside Bangladesh. Final provider and retention disclosures require review before publication.

Account deletion and retention

In the app, open the account screen and choose Remove Account. Because the account is shared, removal affects sign-in to both editions. Uninstalling either app does not delete the account.

Current implementation: removal deletes the Firebase Authentication account, removes selected profile fields (email and Facebook profile fields), and replaces the nickname with “(Account removed)”. It does not automatically erase all historical gameplay, prize, payout, support or other associated records.

For an account or data-deletion request outside the app, contact support@piotr-gorczynski.com. Do not send passwords, wallet PINs or one-time codes.

To be finalized before publication: retention periods, any justified exceptions to deletion, and the operational process for deleting remaining associated data. This draft does not claim that the current removal function deletes all personal data or that a complete retention policy is already implemented.

Questions and changes

Contact support about access, corrections, deletion or this policy. This document is a review draft; an effective date and completed retention/provider disclosures will be supplied before publication.
