Privacy Policy — Bangladesh

Scope and contact

This policy concerns Gridline Soccer Bangladesh (Android package piotr_gorczynski.soccer2.bd), operated by Piotr Górczyński in Poland. It explains how we use personal information to provide the app and its organizer-funded tournament prizes. The Bangladesh edition is for adults aged 18 or older. Contact support@piotr-gorczynski.com about this policy or your information. If you believe a person under 18 is using this edition, please contact us.

Account, authentication and shared profiles

We use Firebase Authentication for email/password, Google, Facebook and anonymous sign-in. Depending on your sign-in method, we process an internal account identifier, email address, nickname and profile information supplied by your sign-in provider, such as a name and profile picture. An anonymous account still has an internal identifier. Authentication information supports sign-in, account recovery and account security.

Global and Bangladesh use the same account and shared profile. We record the app variants used, first and most recent recorded use, and whether use of Bangladesh followed Global. These records describe account usage; they do not establish your physical location or Google Play country. Our app also checks whether the other Gridline Soccer package is installed to enforce the restriction on using both editions on one device.

Gameplay and tournaments

We store friend connections, invitations, online/offline presence, matches, scores, rankings, tournament participation and results in Cloud Firestore and Firebase Realtime Database. These support multiplayer gameplay, invitations, leaderboards and tournament administration. Nicknames and gameplay results are visible to other players through those features. Private payout details are not part of public tournament results.

For prize-tournament registration we record your acceptance of the assigned tournament rules, minimum-age confirmation and confirmation that you have a supported payout account, with timestamps and the relevant requirements. This is a declaration, not an identity-document or location check. Tournament amounts and conditions depend on the assigned rules. Participation does not require an entry fee, stake, wager, deposit, purchase or payment.

Prize recipient information

If you win a prize, we request your first name and last name as separate fields, selected wallet provider (bKash or Rocket) and full mobile wallet number. We use these details to identify the recipient, arrange the prize payment and resolve incorrect, failed or delayed transfers. Remitly requires separate recipient first and last names. We do not request your wallet PIN, password or one-time codes; never include them in a support request.

Prizes are funded by the organizer and processed manually through Remitly. Submitting details does not initiate an automatic transfer. The administrator supplies necessary recipient and transaction information to Remitly and, as necessary to complete the payment, the receiving mobile-money provider, bKash or Rocket. These providers process information under their own notices and may keep their own transaction records.

Payment evidence and support

We distinguish the recipient details needed to execute a transfer from the limited record needed to document a prize. Payment records contain an internal winner identifier, tournament identifier, amount, currency, payout status, processing dates, transfer provider and transaction reference where recorded. Status changes and administrator actions are recorded for accountability and resolving disputes.

Support requests contain your category, message, replies, app context, timestamps and handling history. Please describe the problem without repeating your full wallet number or recipient names when these have already been submitted in the payout form. Support messages and administrator notes can become part of the handling history.

Notifications, analytics, diagnostics and advertising

Firebase Cloud Messaging uses installation and messaging identifiers to deliver invitations, tournament, prize and support notifications. Payment and support notifications use general status messages rather than your full wallet number or the text of your support conversation.

Google Analytics for Firebase processes usage events, app variant, environment, build/version and app/device information to help us understand navigation, sign-in and tournament use and improve the service. Firebase Crashlytics processes crash reports, device/operating-system information and diagnostic breadcrumbs to help find defects. The payout form does not send the entered names or wallet number as analytics event parameters.

Google AdMob displays advertising and may process device information and advertising identifiers. The app uses Google's User Messaging Platform consent flow where applicable. Advertising, analytics and crash diagnostics are distinct forms of processing; an advertising choice does not necessarily disable crash reporting. Use the app's consent/privacy options where available, Android advertising and notification settings, or contact us about your choices.

Recipients and international processing

We use Google Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Realtime Database, Cloud Functions, Cloud Messaging, Analytics and Crashlytics, and Google AdMob. Google or Meta also processes information when you select its sign-in service. Authorized administrators handle payout and support information. Necessary information may also be disclosed to comply with an applicable legal obligation or handle a legal claim. We do not sell personal information.

Retention: recipient details and limited records

Operational recipient details: we retain the separate first name, last name, full wallet number and selected wallet provider while a payout is active and for 180 days after it reaches Completed or Cancelled. Sent means in progress and does not start this period. After that deadline, the automatic daily cleanup deletes the private recipient document and reduces the payment record to limited payment evidence. Deletion occurs on a successful cleanup run, not necessarily at the exact expiry time.

Limited payment and audit records: the minimized payment record and new payment status-history entries are retained for five calendar years from the same terminal-status date, then deleted by the automatic cleanup. This record contains internal winner and tournament identifiers, amount, currency, wallet provider, status, relevant dates and transfer provider/reference where safe to retain. It does not retain recipient first or last names or the full wallet number. Internal identifiers and references remain personal data where they can be linked to a person.

Disputes and legal holds: an unresolved payout support request or an administrator-recorded dispute/legal-obligation hold suspends deletion of both categories. Resolving the request does not override a separate legal hold. Once all holds are released, the original deadlines still apply; overdue information is eligible for the next successful cleanup. Information already deleted cannot be recovered by opening a dispute later.

These are our payout retention rules, not a claim that Bangladesh law requires every full wallet number to be kept for five years. Applicable record-keeping obligations and any justified exceptions must be assessed separately.

Existing history: this change does not delete or migrate older status-history entries, which may contain earlier recipient information. New payment history records metadata only, and new support history refers to messages instead of copying their text. Existing historical data needs a separate reviewed cleanup. Support conversations, other account/game records and copies in other environments or backups are outside this payout cleanup; this policy does not claim that all associated data is automatically erased.

Account deletion and your rights

Open the account screen and choose Remove Account. This deletes the Firebase Authentication account, removes selected profile fields (email and Facebook profile fields) and replaces the nickname with “(Account removed)”. Because the account is shared, removal affects sign-in to both editions. Uninstalling an app does not delete the account.

This action does not immediately erase all associated data: gameplay, presence and support records may remain. Payout recipient details and the minimized payment record follow the 180-day/five-year rules and holds above; account deletion does not restart those periods. Contact support@piotr-gorczynski.com to request review of remaining data, including information no longer needed. Some limited records may need to be retained under applicable law or for legal/accountability purposes; retaining them must not be used as a blanket reason to keep all recipient information.

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to your information, correction, deletion, portability, or object to or seek restriction of processing, and withdraw consent for processing based on consent. Withdrawal does not undo prior lawful processing. We may need proportionate information to verify a request. Do not send passwords or wallet security codes. You may also raise a concern with the competent data-protection authority where that remedy is available.

Security

Authenticated access and database rules restrict payout details to their owner through the app; authorized backend/admin access is used for administration. Other players cannot read your private recipient document through the app's database rules. No system can guarantee absolute security. We aim to limit access and avoid copying payout information into public results, notifications and diagnostics.

It is information about data processing, not a replacement for the shared general Terms or the tournament rules you accept when joining. Material changes will be communicated as required by applicable law. This is a review draft; an effective date will be set after the outstanding operational and legal review.
